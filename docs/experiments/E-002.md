## Experiment: E-002 — Betweenness sample factor 0.4 → 0.2 for faster approximate centrality
- **Date:** 12.09.2026
- **Status:** REJECTED
- **Theory:** `compute_centrality_metrics` routes graphs with >75 nodes through approximate betweenness with k = max(15, 0.4·n). Profiling on synthetic connected graphs (n=100/200) shows betweenness dominates compute time (0.064s/0.238s exact) and halving the factor to 0.2 yields ~4.5–5.4x speedup on the betweenness step while keeping top-10 rank overlap ≥6/10. Sampling theory: betweenness approximation error shrinks with k, but ranking (top-k overlap) is robust to smaller k than absolute values are — so a modest k cut trades little ranking quality for large latency wins on the hot query path.
- **Hypothesis:** H-002: "speedup_quality_score >= 0.90"
